Consent Form Summary:

This research study is exploring how differences in your genes, called single nucleotide polymorphisms or SNPs, affect how well abiraterone works and any side effects you might experience while treating prostate cancer. If you choose to participate, you’ll be randomly assigned to one of two groups: one receiving the usual dose of abiraterone, and the other a lower dose, both alongside prednisone and relugolix for six months, followed by two years of follow-up.  Participation involves blood and urine tests, and imaging scans. There are risks like potential side effects from the drugs, and time spent on extra tests.  However, the study may help improve future cancer treatments. You are free to withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept private, and your insurance will cover the cost of standard tests. While there is no pay for participation, if you are injured, your insurance may cover medical costs.  For questions, contact Dr. Han Xu at 1-301-222-7143. An optional part of the study involves storing your blood sample for future research, with strict privacy protections, and you can choose not to participate in this aspect.

## What are the study groups?

This study has two study groups.

- Group 1 will get the usual chemotherapy high dose drugs to treat this type of cancer: ZYTIGA - Abiraterone plus DELTASONE - Prednisone plus ORGOVYX - Relugolix:
- o ZYTIGA - Abiraterone Tablet -- 2 x 500 mg tablets orally once daily (12 weeks)
- o DELTASONE - Prednisone Tablet -- 5 mg orally twice daily (12 weeks)
- o ORGOVYX- Relugolix Tablet -- 360 mg orally first day -- 120 mg orally once daily (12 weeks)
- o This chemotherapy will be 90 days.
- Group 2 will get the study chemotherapy low dose drugs to treat this type of cancer: ZYTIGA - Abiraterone plus DELTASONE - Prednisone plus ORGOVYX - Relugolix:
- o ZYTIGA - Abiraterone Acetate -- 4 x 250 mg tablets orally once daily (12 weeks)
- o DELTASONE - Prednisone tablet -- 5 mg orally once daily (12 weeks)
- o ORGOVYX- Relugolix Tablet -- 360 mg orally first day --- 120 mg orally once daily (12 weeks)
- o This chemotherapy will be 90 days.

A  computer  will  by  chance  assign  you  to  treatment  groups  in  the  study.  This  is  called randomization.  This is done by chance because no one knows if one study group is better or worse than the other. Neither you nor your doctor can choose which group you will be in.
