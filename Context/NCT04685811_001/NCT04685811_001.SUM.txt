Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if two different methods of producing Gallium-68 (68Ga) for PET/CT scans provide equally clear images of metastatic prostate cancer. Approximately 30 participants with prostate cancer will be enrolled, and you will undergo two PET/CT scans, 6 to 48 hours apart, each lasting about 75 minutes. These scans involve a small injection of a radioactive tracer and may include blood draws before, during, and after the scan. Potential risks include minor discomfort from the injection, exposure to a low level of radiation (similar to standard CT scans), and a very small chance of allergic reaction. There may be unexpected findings on the scans that require further evaluation at your own expense. Participation is entirely voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without affecting your regular medical care. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and access to it is limited and protected by law. You will not be compensated for participating, but all study-related procedures are covered. If you experience an injury related to the study, immediate medical care will be provided at usual charges, but no monetary compensation is available. For questions or concerns, contact Dr. Joseph R. Osborne at 212-746-6797 or the WCMC IRB Office at (646) 962-8200.
