Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to understand how a new drug, called a radiotracer, moves through the body and attaches to cells, using a special camera called a PET Scanner. You are invited to participate if you are either healthy or have been diagnosed with Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) and are starting immunotherapy treatment. Participation involves 3-5 visits lasting 4-7 hours total, with up to 8 people participating at UC Davis. You will receive a PET/CT scan, and those with NSCLC may have a second scan after starting treatment. While there’s no direct benefit to you, the study may help future cancer patients. Risks include potential anxiety from the scanner, possible incidental findings requiring follow-up, and minor injection-related discomfort. Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your privacy will be protected, and your medical records will be kept confidential. You will be compensated $100 per scan, and medical care is available if you are injured during the study. Contact Simon R. Cherry, Ph.D., or Megan Daly, MD, at 916-731-9004 with questions, or the IRB at (916) 703-9151 if you have concerns about your rights. There is no cost to you for the research scans themselves, but you’ll be responsible for expenses like transportation.
