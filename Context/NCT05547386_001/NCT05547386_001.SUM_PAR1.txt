Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to provide access to a special imaging scan, called a 68 Ga-PSMA-11 PET/CT scan, for patients being considered for prostate cancer treatment. You’re being asked to participate because your doctors are evaluating you for a specific therapy. The study involves having one scan, which takes about 2-3 hours, and exposes you to a small amount of radiation with a low risk of harmful effects. Common side effects may include nausea, diarrhea, or dizziness. While there may be no direct benefit to you, the scan could help determine if you’re a good candidate for treatment. You can choose to stop participating at any time without penalty. Your privacy is protected through coded information, and while data may be used for future research, your identity will remain confidential. Mayo Clinic will cover costs for injuries directly related to the study, beyond what your insurance covers. Participation is voluntary, and you can contact Dr. Geoffrey Johnson at 507-284-4104 or Amber Stephan at 507-266-2927 with questions. You won’t be paid for participating, but won’t have to pay for the scan itself.
