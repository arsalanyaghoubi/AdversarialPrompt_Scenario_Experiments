Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine a safe dosage of an HPV vaccine, alone or combined with a drug called M7824, and to see if this treatment can shrink tumors in adults with advanced HPV-associated cancer that has returned after treatment or for whom standard treatment isn’t suitable. You may receive either the HPV vaccine alone (Group A) or the vaccine with M7824 (Group B). The study involves regular injections of the HPV vaccine and infusions of M7824 every two weeks, along with frequent monitoring through blood tests, scans, and physical exams over approximately one year. Possible side effects include fatigue, nausea, skin reactions, bleeding, and more serious issues affecting organs like the liver or heart; a full list is provided in the consent. While you may not directly benefit, the study could help others in the future. You can choose to stop participating at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, though it may be shared for research purposes with safeguards in place. The NIH will cover some travel and lodging costs, but won’t pay for long-term care or injuries. If you have questions or concerns, contact Dr. Charalampos Floudas at 240-858-3032 or charalampos.floudas@nih.gov, or the NIH Clinical Center Patient Representative at 301-496-2626. Your participation is voluntary and your decision will be respected.
