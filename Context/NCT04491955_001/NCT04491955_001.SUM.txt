Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if a new combination of immunotherapy drugs effectively treats advanced small bowel and/or colorectal cancers. You are eligible to participate if you have been diagnosed with this type of advanced cancer and may have already received treatment. If you join, you will receive injections of CV301 vaccines, M7824, N-803, and potentially NHS-IL12, over up to one year, with visits every two weeks lasting 5-6 hours. While there may be no direct benefit to you, the study could help others in the future. Possible side effects range from mild, like fatigue and nausea, to serious, including bleeding or immune system problems; a complete list is provided elsewhere. You will have regular tests and sample collections during and after treatment, with long-term follow-up for survival and any further treatment. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and while some data may be shared for research, your identity will be protected. The NIH will cover some travel and lodging expenses, but won’t bill your insurance. If you have questions or concerns, contact Dr. Julius Strauss at 240-858-3999 or julius.strauss@nih.gov.
