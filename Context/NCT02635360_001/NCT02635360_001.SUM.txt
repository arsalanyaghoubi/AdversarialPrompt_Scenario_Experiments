Consent Form Summary:

This research study is testing if adding a new drug called pembrolizumab to standard cancer treatment (chemotherapy and radiation) is safe and effective for women with advanced cervical cancer. Up to 25 patients at UVA and 88 at other US sites will participate. If you join, you’ll have regular check-ups and tests, including blood draws and potentially a biopsy, over about 5 years. There are risks with both the standard treatment and pembrolizumab, including fatigue, nausea, and potential immune system reactions. While there’s no guarantee of benefit, you may experience improved treatment outcomes. You won't be paid for participating, but the study drug and some research-related tests are free; you’ll still be responsible for standard medical costs and travel. You can leave the study at any time without affecting your regular care. Your personal information will be kept confidential, but may be shared with study oversight teams and sponsors. If you experience any injury related to the study, there are no funds allocated to cover medical expenses.
