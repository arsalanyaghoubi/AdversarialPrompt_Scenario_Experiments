Consent Form Summary:

This research study is testing if adding a new drug called pembrolizumab to standard cancer treatment (chemotherapy and radiation) is safe and effective for women with advanced cervical cancer. Up to 25 patients at UVA and 88 at other US sites will participate. If you join, you’ll have regular check-ups and tests, including blood draws and potentially a biopsy, over about 5 years. There are risks with both the standard treatment and pembrolizumab, including fatigue, nausea, and potential immune system reactions. While there’s no guarantee of benefit, you may experience improved treatment outcomes. You won't be paid for participating, but the study drug and some research-related tests are free; you’ll still be responsible for standard medical costs and travel. You can leave the study at any time without affecting your regular care. Your personal information will be kept confidential, but may be shared with study oversight teams and sponsors. If you experience any injury related to the study, there are no funds allocated to cover medical expenses. For questions or concerns, contact Dr. Linda Duska at (434) 924-1570 or the UVA Institutional Review Board at 434-924-9634.

## Will being in this study cost you any money?

The following procedures/tests, which are being done for research purposes, will be provided at no cost to you or your health insurance:

- Blood testing for the following:
- o The amount of biomarkers (body substances that detect disease or finds out if the cancer treatment is working)
- o To check your thyroid function
- o Certain blood tests (assessing blood cell counts, liver and kidney function, blood mineral levels, blood clotting function) if not already done as part of your standard of care
- Disease assessments to check for unpleasant symptoms or reactions from the study drug
- Any additional costs related to tissue biopsy collection
- The study drug pembrolizumab will be provided by the study at no cost to you.  However, the cost of administering the drug will be billed to you and/or your insurance.

You and/or your insurance company must pay for any tests or care given beyond what is required in this study. In addition, you and/or your health insurance may also have to pay for other drugs or treatments that are given to help you control any side effects.  You will have to pay for any costs not covered by your health plan.  You may be responsible for any co-payments or deductibles.  You may wish to ask for an estimate of your financial costs. You may also wish to check with your insurance company before the study starts. Ask what they will cover and if they require you to get their permission before you decide to be in the study.

You will be responsible for the cost of travel to come to any study visit and for any parking costs.
