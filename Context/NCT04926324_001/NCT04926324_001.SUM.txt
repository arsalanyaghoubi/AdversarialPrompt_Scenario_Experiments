Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine the best dose of niraparib when combined with radiation and dostarlimab for treating locally advanced rectal cancer. Up to 55 participants will be involved, and your active participation will last approximately 15 weeks, including treatment and follow-up for up to 2 years, and potentially lifelong monitoring. The study involves blood tests, scans (PET/CT, MRI, urinalysis), a flexible sigmoidoscopy, and receiving niraparib, dostarlimab infusions, and radiation therapy. Potential risks include side effects from the drugs and radiation, such as low blood cell counts, infections, high blood pressure, and allergic reactions; serious risks are possible and require immediate medical attention. While there’s no guarantee of benefit, the study may help improve future treatments for rectal cancer. Alternatives include standard radiation therapy alone or other clinical trials. Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, but may be shared with regulatory agencies and the study sponsor, GlaxoSmithKline. You won’t be billed for the study drugs, hepatitis testing, or the sigmoidoscopy, but will be responsible for standard healthcare costs like co-pays, travel, and follow-up care. You will not be paid for participation. If you experience an injury related to the study, standard medical care is available, but the University of Iowa typically doesn’t cover additional costs unless due to negligence.
