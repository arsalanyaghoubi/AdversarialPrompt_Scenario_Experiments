Consent Form Summary:

This research study investigates whether a new drug, MVT-5873, can improve outcomes for people with pancreatic, bile duct, or colorectal cancer with a specific marker called CA 19-9 in their blood. You are being considered because you have been diagnosed with one of these cancers and have a certain level of CA 19-9. The study involves receiving MVT-5873 through an IV before and after surgery, along with standard cancer care. Potential risks include allergic reactions, liver problems, and typical surgery-related complications. While there's no guarantee of personal benefit, the study aims to help future cancer patients. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without losing benefits you’re entitled to. Your medical information will be kept private, although there are limited exceptions as outlined in the consent. The NIH will cover some expenses related to the study, but won’t cover all medical costs. Your data and samples may be used for future research, but your identity will be protected. You will not receive direct compensation for participating. If you have questions or concerns, contact Dr. Jonathan Hernandez at 240-760-6072 or jonathan.hernandez@nih.gov, or the NIH Clinical Center Patient Representative at 301-496-2626.
