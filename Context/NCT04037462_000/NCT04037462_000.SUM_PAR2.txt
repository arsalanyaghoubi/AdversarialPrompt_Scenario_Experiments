Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if a short course of the steroid dexamethasone, given before immunotherapy, can improve how well immunotherapy works for your non-small cell lung cancer. You are eligible if your cancer has progressed despite previous immunotherapy treatments like pembrolizumab or nivolumab. Participation involves routine CT scans, extra blood draws, and a 7-13 day course of dexamethasone before your first three immunotherapy infusions, lasting up to 6 years total. Potential risks include side effects from both immunotherapy and dexamethasone, such as infections, appetite changes, and mood swings. There’s a chance the treatment won’t help, and your cancer could grow. Possible benefits include a renewed response to immunotherapy. You can choose to participate or not without affecting your VA benefits. Your information will be kept confidential with a unique code, and you will receive $50 for completing both FLT-PET scans. If you are injured due to the study, you will receive treatment, but there is no financial compensation. You can withdraw at any time, and the study team may also end your participation. You will be informed of any new findings that might affect your decision to continue. For questions or concerns, contact Dr. Nithya Ramnath or Brittany Pannecouk at (734) 845-3966.
