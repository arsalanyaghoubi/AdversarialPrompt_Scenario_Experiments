Consent Form Summary:

This research study is exploring the safety and potential benefits of the drug Epidiolex in men with prostate cancer whose PSA levels have risen after previous treatment with surgery or radiation. The study will last about 120 days and involves six visits to the University of Kentucky Medical Center, each lasting around three hours. You’ll be asked to take Epidiolex daily and have regular check-ups, including blood tests and physical exams. Potential risks include appetite changes, diarrhea, fatigue, mood changes, and allergic reactions; a full list is in the detailed consent. While there’s no guarantee of benefit, the study could help improve future prostate cancer treatments and may lower your PSA or testosterone levels. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and while there is no compensation for participation, the study drug and related tests will be provided at no cost to you. If you are injured during the study, the University of Kentucky does not have funds to cover medical costs. Contact Dr. Zin Myint at 859-323-3964 with any questions or concerns, or the UK Office of Research Integrity at 859-257-9428 for questions about your rights as a research participant.
