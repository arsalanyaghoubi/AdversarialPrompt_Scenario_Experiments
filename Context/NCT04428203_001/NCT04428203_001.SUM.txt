Consent Form Summary:

This research study is exploring the safety and potential benefits of the drug Epidiolex in men with prostate cancer whose PSA levels have risen after previous treatment with surgery or radiation. The study will last about 120 days and involve six visits to the University of Kentucky Medical Center, each lasting around 3 hours. You’ll be asked to take Epidiolex daily, have blood and urine tests, and undergo physical exams and scans to monitor your health and cancer. Potential risks include appetite changes, diarrhea, fatigue, mood changes, and allergic reactions. While there’s no guarantee of benefit, the study could help doctors learn more about using Epidiolex for prostate cancer. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and if you are injured due to the study, the University of Kentucky does not have funds to cover related costs. Contact Dr. Zin Myint at 859-323-3964 with any questions, and you’ll receive a copy of this consent form. You won’t be paid for participating, but the study drug and related tests will be covered. Your samples and information will not be used for future research without further consent.
