Consent Form Summary:

This research study (NCT01064466) aims to compare a study chemotherapy drug to the usual chemotherapy drug, Etoposide Capsule, for small cell lung cancer. You’re being asked to participate because you’ve had a lung tissue biopsy. The study will randomly assign you to receive either the usual chemotherapy or the study chemotherapy, given orally for six months, with follow-up for two years. Extra tests, including blood, urine, and tissue tests, will be performed. Potential risks include time away from work, side effects like infection or nausea, and possible effects on your heart, liver, or blood. Possible benefits are uncertain, but the study may help future patients. You can stop participating at any time. Your medical information will be kept private, though it may be shared with organizations overseeing the study. There are no costs to you, but you won’t be paid. If you’re injured, your insurance may cover treatment. You can contact Han Xu, M.D., Ph.D. at 1-301-222-7143 with any questions. Additionally, an optional biobanking study is available, where your samples may be stored for future research, with the option to decline participation.

## What extra tests will I have if I take part in this study?

If you are assigned to Group 1, all of the tests you will have are part of the usual approach to curing your cancer.  If  you  are  assigned  to  Group  2,  all  of  the  tests  you  will  have  are  part  of  the  study approach for your cancer. There are blood, urine, and tissue tests that you will need to have:

- Sequence precisely each target gene DNA whole chain in peripheral blood as follows: DNA Topoisomerase II.
- Correlate Topoisomerase II Gene Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (SNPs) to Using Etoposide Therapeutic Effects of Treating Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC), based on precisely sequencing drug target gene via Peripheral Blood DNA.
- Sequence precisely each target gene DNA whole chain in peripheral blood as follows: CYP450 3A4.
- Correlate CYP450 3A4 Gene Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (SNPs) to Using Etoposide Side Effects of Treating Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC), based on precisely sequencing drug target gene via Peripheral Blood DNA.
- You will need a lung tissue biopsy test at least one time.
- You will need an image test at least one time per month.
- If you have an allergic reaction to oncology drugs, you will have an extra blood test.
- You will need urine tests every week while you are taking oncology drugs.
- You will need blood tests every week while you are taking oncology drugs.

Your insurance  company will be  charged for the  blood,  urine,  and  tissue  biopsy  tests  related  to oncology drugs.
