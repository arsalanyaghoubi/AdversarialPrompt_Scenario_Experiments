Consent Form Summary:

This research study is exploring whether a special type of PET scan, called 68 Ga-PSMA-11, can help surgeons better locate and remove prostate cancer. If you agree to participate, you’ll be one of 55 people involved. You’ll receive a single injection of a small amount of a radioactive substance, followed by a PET/CT scan to create images of your body.  The study will last about two years, including follow-up appointments after surgery to assess your quality of life – things like urinary and sexual function. While the scan may help improve surgical planning, there’s no guarantee you’ll directly benefit. Potential risks include a very small chance of allergic reaction to the injection, minor bruising from the IV, and exposure to radiation (about the amount you’d get from 3-4 years of natural background radiation). Your personal information will be kept confidential, and you can withdraw from the study at any time without penalty. You won’t be charged for the research scan, but standard medical costs will apply. If you experience a research-related injury, medical treatment will be provided, but you’re responsible for costs not covered by insurance. For questions or concerns, contact Dr. Bahler at 317-944-3458 or the IU Human Research Protection Program at 800-696-2949.
