Consent Form Summary:

This research study, called ‘LiveWell’, is testing a skills training program to help people living with advanced lung cancer cope with difficult emotions and symptoms like fatigue, pain, and breathlessness. About 30 participants will be involved, and you’re being asked to participate because you have advanced lung cancer. If you join, you’ll complete three assessments and eight one-on-one training sessions – either in person or through video conferencing – over approximately 12 weeks, learning coping skills from a therapy called dialectical behavioral therapy. While there’s no direct medical benefit, you may feel better equipped to manage your symptoms. Participation involves a small risk of feeling increased distress from session topics or surveys. Your information will be kept confidential, and you'll be compensated up to $150 for your time. You are free to withdraw at any time without penalty, and medical care for any study-related injury is available. If you have questions, contact Tamara Somers, PhD at 919-416-3408 or the Duke University IRB at (919) 668-5111. There are no costs to you for participating, and the study may be terminated if new risks are discovered or if the program isn't showing positive results.
