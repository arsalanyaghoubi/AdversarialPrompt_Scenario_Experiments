Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to improve the quality of life and appetite of cancer patients experiencing loss of appetite during treatment. If you agree to participate, you will be one of about 30 patients with head/neck, lung, or pancreatic cancer – or metastatic disease – undergoing radiation, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or targeted therapy. Over three months, your appetite will be assessed using a questionnaire, and you’ll receive nutrition counseling, potentially alongside an appetite stimulant like medical marijuana, mirtazapine, or dexamethasone. While there are potential side effects from these stimulants – such as drowsiness or dizziness – there are no significant physical risks to participation. You may experience improved appetite and receive personalized nutrition guidance. Participation is completely voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without affecting your medical care. Your medical records will be reviewed, and information collected will be kept confidential. Your data may be stored long-term and used for future research with your consent, though you can opt-out of this data banking. There is no payment for participation, but your insurance or you will cover the cost of any appetite stimulant used. If you experience an injury related to the study, basic medical treatment will be provided, but further care will be billed to your insurance or you.
