Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine the safety and effectiveness of combining two drugs, grapiprant (IK-007) and eribulin, for treating metastatic inflammatory breast cancer (mIBC) – cancer that has spread. Participation is voluntary, and you can stop at any time without penalty. Before joining, you’ll undergo tests like blood draws, scans, and potentially a biopsy to check your eligibility. If eligible, you’ll receive the study drugs over cycles, with regular check-ups to monitor your health. Potential risks include side effects like fatigue, nausea, low blood counts, and heart or liver problems; the full list is available for review. While there may be no direct benefit to you, the study could help future patients. Grapiprant will be provided at no cost, but you’ll be responsible for eribulin costs, potentially covered by insurance. You may receive up to $600 in reimbursement for travel expenses. Your personal information will be kept confidential, and data/samples may be used for future research, though you can request they not be. If you experience an injury directly related to the study, standard medical care will be billed as usual.
