Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if a special imaging scan, called an 18F-fluciclovine PET-CT scan, can better track changes in prostate cancer tumors. You will receive two of these research scans, alongside your usual medical tests. Participation involves an injection of a radioactive agent and a 30-minute scan. While generally safe, potential risks include pain or redness at the injection site, dizziness, or, rarely, allergic reactions. The scan exposes you to radiation, so precautions like limiting contact with pregnant women and infants for two hours after the scan are recommended. There are no direct benefits to you, but future patients may benefit from what we learn. Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and study-related scans are provided at no cost to you. If you experience an injury directly related to the study, your medical care will be billed as usual. You won’t be compensated for participation or potential discoveries, and the study may be stopped at any time. Contact Dr. Gregory Ravizzini at 713-745-5721 or the IRB at 713-792-6477 with questions. Your data may be used in future research, with efforts made to remove identifying information.
