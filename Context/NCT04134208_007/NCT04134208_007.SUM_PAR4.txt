Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if a special imaging scan, called an 18F-fluciclovine PET-CT scan, can better track changes in prostate cancer tumors. You will receive two of these research scans, alongside your usual medical tests. Participation involves an injection of a radioactive agent and a 30-minute scan. While generally safe, potential risks include pain or redness at the injection site, dizziness, or, rarely, allergic reactions. The scan exposes you to radiation, so precautions like limiting contact with pregnant women and infants for two hours after the scan are recommended. There are no direct benefits to you, but future patients may benefit from what we learn. Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, and study-related scans are provided at no cost to you. If you experience an injury directly related to the study, your medical care will be billed as usual. You won’t be compensated for participation or potential discoveries, and the study may be stopped at any time. Contact Dr. Gregory Ravizzini at 713-745-5721 or the IRB at 713-792-6477 with questions. Your data may be used in future research, with efforts made to remove identifying information.

## STUDY SUMMARY

The goal of this clinical research study is to learn if a radioactive imaging agent called 18 F-fluciclovine used during a PET-CT scan can further help measure changes in tumor size. The results of these scans will be compared to standard of care imaging scans.

This is an investigational study. 18 F-fluciclovine is an FDA-approved and commercially available radioactive imaging agent for PET/CT scans in adult males with suspected prostate cancer. The PET-CT scans are performed using FDA approved and commercially available methods. The use of  18 F-fluciclovine to measure changes in tumor size in this study is investigational.

Future patients may benefit from what is learned from your participation in this study. There are no direct benefits for you in this study.

Your participation is completely voluntary. Before choosing to take part in this study, you should discuss with the study team any concerns you may have, including side effects, potential expenses, and time commitment. If you take part in this study, you may experience pain or redness at the injection site or a change in taste.

You can read a list of potential side effects below in the Possible Risks section of this consent.

Your active participation in this study will be over after the post-treatment  18 Ffluciclovine PET-CT scan.

The  18 F-fluciclovine PET-CT scans will be provided at no cost to you while taking part in this study.

You may choose not to take part in this study.
