Consent Form Summary:

This research study is testing if adding a new drug, niraparib, to standard prostate cancer treatment (hormones and radiation) can help prevent the cancer from returning. You are being asked to participate because you have prostate cancer with a high chance of recurrence. If you choose to join, you’ll receive usual hormone therapy and radiation, plus the study drug niraparib for up to a year. The study will involve regular blood tests, blood pressure checks, and collection of tissue and blood samples for research. There are potential risks like fatigue, low blood counts, high blood pressure, and side effects from the drugs and radiation; some risks are currently unknown. Possible benefits include shrinking or stabilizing the cancer, though this is not certain. You can stop participating at any time without affecting your regular medical care. Your medical information will be kept private, though it may be shared for research purposes with safeguards in place. There is no cost to you for the study drug, but you will be responsible for usual care costs and potential travel/time off work. You won’t be paid for participation, but your samples may contribute to future discoveries. If you are injured during the study, insurance may not cover the costs.
