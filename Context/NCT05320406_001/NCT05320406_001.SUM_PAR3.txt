Consent Form Summary:

This research study is investigating how prostate cancer treatment, specifically hormone therapy, affects the heart. If you are diagnosed with prostate cancer and receiving radiation therapy, potentially with hormone therapy, you may be eligible to participate. The study will involve standard radiation treatment and blood tests, plus two additional heart imaging scans – one before and one after treatment – over 12 months. You may be randomly assigned to receive either standard hormone therapy (like Lupron) or a newer option, relugolix. While the study isn’t designed to directly benefit you, it aims to improve future prostate cancer care. Potential risks include discomfort from blood draws, allergic reaction to contrast dye used in imaging, and the typical side effects of hormone therapy. You will receive $100 for each completed heart scan to compensate for your time. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without affecting your medical care. Your privacy will be protected through assigned study numbers and secure data handling. If you experience an injury related to the study, medical care will be provided, but insurance will generally cover the costs. ## Medical Record

If you have been an Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital patient before, then you already have an Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital medical record. If you have never been an Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital patient, you do not have one. An Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital medical record will be made for you if an Emory Atlanta and Saint Joseph's Hospital provider or facility gives you any services or procedures for this study.

Copies of the consent form/HIPAA authorization that you sign will be put in any Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital medical record you have now or any time during the study.

Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital may create study information about you that can help with your care. For example, the results of study tests or procedures. These study results will be put in your Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital medical record. Anyone who has access to your medical records will be able to have access to all the study information placed there. The confidentiality of the study information in your medical record will be protected by laws like the HIPAA privacy rule. State and federal laws may not protect the research information from disclosure.

The results of some study tests and procedures will be used only for research purposes and will not be placed in your medical record. For this study, those items include: cardiac CT imaging results, research blood studies.

Tests and procedures done at non-Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital places may not become part of your Emory and Saint Joseph's Hospital medical record. Also, if you decide to be in this study, it is up to you to let your other health providers know.
