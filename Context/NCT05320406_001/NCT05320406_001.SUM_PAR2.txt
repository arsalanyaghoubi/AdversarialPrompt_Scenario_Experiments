Consent Form Summary:

This research study is investigating how prostate cancer treatment, specifically hormone therapy, affects the heart. If you are diagnosed with prostate cancer and receiving radiation therapy, potentially with hormone therapy, you may be eligible to participate. The study will involve standard radiation treatment and blood tests, plus two additional heart imaging scans – one before and one after treatment – over 12 months. You may be randomly assigned to receive either standard hormone therapy (like Lupron) or a newer option, relugolix. While the study isn’t designed to directly benefit you, it aims to improve future prostate cancer care. Potential risks include discomfort from blood draws, allergic reaction to contrast dye used in imaging, and the typical side effects of hormone therapy. You will receive $100 for each completed heart scan to compensate for your time. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without affecting your medical care. Your privacy will be protected through assigned study numbers and secure data handling. If you experience an injury related to the study, medical care will be provided, but insurance will generally cover the costs. For questions or concerns, contact Dr. Sagar Patel at [contact information]. You can also reach the Emory Institutional Review Board at [contact information].

## Cost

The study sponsor will pay for certain items and services that you may receive if you take part in this study.

You will have to pay for the items or services for which the study sponsor does not pay. The sponsor will






not pay for your regular medical care. If you have insurance, Emory will submit claims to your insurance for items and services that the sponsor does not cover. Emory will send in only those claims for items and services that it reasonably believes your insurance will pay and that the sponsor has not paid.

The actual amount that you have to pay depends on whether or not you have health insurance and whether or not that insurance will pay for any research study costs. Generally, insurance companies will not pay for items and services that are required just for a research study. Some insurance companies will not pay for regular medical treatment or treatment for complications if you are in a study. How much you will have to pay for any co-payments, deductibles or co-insurance depends on your plan. Emory and the sponsor will not pay for these costs.

It is a good idea to contact your insurance provider and tell them you want to be in this research study. Ask them what they will pay for and what they will not pay for. You can also ask the study team for help in figuring out what you will have to pay.

If you do not have insurance, Emory will review your case as part of its program for low-income patient care. The standard policies of that program will apply. The program will figure out if you have to pay any costs for taking part in the study and what those costs will be.

Standard hormone therapy with leuprolide (e.g. Lupron) will not be covered by the study and will be billed to the participant/insurance.
