Consent Form Summary:

This research study is investigating how prostate cancer treatment, specifically hormone therapy, affects the heart. If you are diagnosed with prostate cancer and receiving radiation therapy, potentially with hormone therapy, you may be eligible to participate. The study will involve standard radiation treatment and blood tests, plus two additional heart imaging scans – one before and one after treatment – over 12 months. You may be randomly assigned to receive either standard hormone therapy (like Lupron) or a newer option, relugolix. While the study isn’t designed to directly benefit you, it aims to improve future prostate cancer care. Potential risks include discomfort from blood draws, allergic reaction to contrast dye used in imaging, and the typical side effects of hormone therapy. You will receive $100 for each completed heart scan to compensate for your time. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without affecting your medical care. Your privacy will be protected through assigned study numbers and secure data handling. If you experience an injury related to the study, medical care will be provided, but insurance will generally cover the costs.
