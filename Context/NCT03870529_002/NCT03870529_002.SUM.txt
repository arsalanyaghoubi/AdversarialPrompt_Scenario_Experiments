Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to see if vitamin A can boost the immune system’s ability to fight non-small cell lung cancer by increasing germinal centers in tumors. If you’re eligible, you’ll be randomly assigned to either receive vitamin A for 7 days before surgery, or proceed directly to surgery – both groups will have surgery as part of their standard care. Participation involves blood draws and taking vitamin A if assigned to that group, lasting up to 3 months. Potential risks include side effects from vitamin A like bleeding gums, headache, or diarrhea, though these are uncommon. There’s no guaranteed benefit from participating. You can choose to withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, though it may be shared for research purposes and regulatory review. Parking will be validated for study visits, but you won’t receive additional payment. If you experience an injury related to the study, limited insurance coverage is available.
