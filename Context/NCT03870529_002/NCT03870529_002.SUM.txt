Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if vitamin A can boost the immune system’s ability to fight non-small cell lung cancer by increasing germinal centers in tumors. If you are eligible, you’ll be randomly assigned to receive either vitamin A for 7 days before surgery, which is standard care for your condition, or to proceed directly to surgery without the vitamin. Participation involves two visits over approximately 3 months and includes blood draws. Potential risks of vitamin A include mouth sores, headache, and, rarely, more serious side effects. There’s no guarantee of benefit from participating. You have the right to withdraw at any time without penalty. Your medical information will be kept confidential, though it may be shared with study teams and regulatory agencies as required by law.  Limited insurance coverage of up to $25,000 is available for study-related injuries. Parking will be validated for study visits, but you won’t receive any other payment.
