Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to determine if patients receiving chemotherapy with paclitaxel (Taxol™) can safely stop taking medications used to prevent allergic reactions. If you enroll, you’ll receive standard chemotherapy treatment, and after the first two doses, you’ll be randomly assigned to either continue or discontinue those preventative medications. You will complete brief surveys before and after each chemotherapy dose to track your symptoms and quality of life, taking approximately 8-10 hours over 2-4 months. Potential risks include allergic reactions to paclitaxel, similar to those experienced by patients not in the study, and possible side effects from the preventative medications if you continue receiving them. There may be no direct benefit to you, but the study could help future patients. You can stop participating at any time without penalty. Your medical records will be kept confidential, and your participation is voluntary. If you experience an injury related to the study, medical care will be provided, but costs may be billed to you or your insurance. For questions about the study, contact Michael Berger at 614-293-0191 or Michael.Berger@osumc.edu; for privacy concerns, contact Kathleen Ojala at 614-293-6482 or Kathleen.Ojala@osumc.edu; or for participant rights, contact the Office of Responsible Research Practices at 1-800-678-6251. You will not be paid for participating, but may incur small costs for text message surveys depending on your phone plan.

## 14. Will my study-related information be kept confidential?

We will work to make sure that no one sees your survey responses without approval. But, because REDCap uses the Internet, there is a chance that someone could access your online responses without permission. In some cases, this information could be used to identify you. Your data will be protected with a code to reduce the risk that other people can view the responses.

Efforts will be made to keep your study-related information confidential.  However, there may be circumstances where this information must be released.  For example, personal information regarding your participation in this study may be disclosed if required by state law.







Also, your records may be reviewed by the following groups (as applicable to the research):

- Office for Human Research Protections or other federal, state, or international regulatory agencies;
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration;
- The Ohio State University Institutional Review Board or Office of Responsible Research Practices;
- The sponsor supporting the study, their agents or study monitors; and
- Your insurance company (if charges are billed to insurance).

A description of this clinical trial will be available on http://www.ClinicalTrials.gov, as required by U.S. law.  This website will not include information that can identify you.  At most, the website will include a summary of the results.  You can search the website at any time.
