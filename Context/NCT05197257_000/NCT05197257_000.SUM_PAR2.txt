Consent Form Summary:

This research study aims to improve prostate cancer detection using a special imaging agent called 68Ga-PSMA-11 with PET scans. Up to 100 patients will participate, building on the experience of thousands who’ve already had this imaging safely. If you join, you’ll receive an intravenous injection of a small amount of a radioactive substance, followed by a PET/CT or PET/MRI scan lasting about two hours. There are potential risks like pain or bruising from the IV, radiation exposure similar to five years of natural background radiation, and possible side effects from the MRI, including warmth or flashing lights. Potential benefits include providing your doctor with valuable information for your care. Alternatives exist, but this specific imaging agent isn’t widely available locally yet. Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw anytime without penalty. Your privacy will be protected, though complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Medical care is available for study-related injuries, but you or your insurance will likely cover the costs. You won’t be paid for participating, but the study covers costs not typically billed to insurance. If you have questions, contact Dr. Bennett Chin at 720-848-6137. Your data and samples may be used for future research with IRB approval.
