Consent Form Summary:

This research study, Winship4517-18, led by Dr. Olatunji Alese, is testing a new combination of two drugs, Niraparib and Panitumumab, for people with advanced colon or rectal cancer. The goal is to see if this combination is safe and effective. If you choose to participate, you’ll be one of approximately 26 patients, and participation will last at least 8 weeks, potentially longer if you are benefiting from the treatment. You'll have regular check-ups every two weeks, including blood tests, physical exams, and scans to monitor your cancer. Potential risks include side effects from the drugs like low blood cell counts, high blood pressure, and digestive issues; a full list is available in the consent form. While you won't directly benefit from the study, it could help others in the future. Your medical care will continue as usual, regardless of your decision to participate. You are free to withdraw at any time without penalty. Your health information will be kept confidential, and while the study sponsor (Tesaro/GlaxoSmithKline) will have access, it will be protected. Emory will provide medical care if you experience injury related to the study, though costs may be billed to your insurance.
